Pull a Hop from The Hop Chronicles

The index of 106 posts is built into this file, so looking up a URL works offline — it resolves the variety, crop year, origin, series and publication date with no network at all. Fetching only adds the descriptor ratings and the measured alpha acid of that lot.

Fetching will usually fail. Brülosophy does not send CORS headers, and a browser will not let this page read a cross‑site response without them — that is the site's decision, not something this file can work around. Opening the post and pasting its text does the same job and always works. Nothing is ever saved automatically; every parsed value lands in the form for you to check.

How scores are derived

The series uses a consistent results sentence, so scores come from the post's own ranking rather than a guess:

Where the descriptor appearsScore
1st in the "most prominent" list9/10
2nd / 3rd / 4th+8 / 7 / 6
Mentioned elsewhere in the post5/10
Never mentioned2/10
Named among the "lowest rated"1/10

This is a ranking translated to a scale, not a measurement — Brülosophy publishes descriptor votes, not oil analysis. Two hops both scoring 9 on citrus were each their post's top descriptor; it does not mean they are equally citrusy. Descriptors the series tracks but this tool has no axis for (onion/garlic, apple/pear) are reported as notes instead of being folded into a neighbouring axis.

Where each profile comes from. Every comparison shows a badge. composite and estimate are generalized variety characters — informed judgement about how a hop reads, not measurement. reported is second-hand: the descriptors were taken from search-engine summaries of a Brülosophy post, not from the post itself. Treat these as provisional. The same channel was caught repeating one hop's lab figures for two others, and 12 of these 14 lead with the same citrus-and-tropical pair, which is either genuine or a summariser repeating itself — there is no way to tell without the page. from post is first-hand: you pasted the article text into the Hop Chronicles tab and the descriptors were read out of it. Doing that upgrades a reported entry. unscored means no profile exists; the entry is excluded from the radar, the blend average and the ranking. A few obscure experimentals have no reliable public character to draw on, and are left blank rather than guessed.

Cross-checked vs verified. Cross-checked means the alpha and beta figures were reconciled against a second hop database and the entry names which record it matched — a real citation, but still someone else's compiled figures rather than a measurement of your lot. Verified is the stronger claim and only you can set it. A hop can be cross-checked and still unverified.

Verified vs assumed. Nothing ships verified — only you can attest to a source. When you check a hop against a spec sheet, a lab report or your own lot, tick verified under Manage Hops and name what you checked it against; the entry is then marked \u2713 everywhere it appears, and a later data update will never overwrite it with an estimate. The library panel tracks how much of your library is confirmed rather than assumed.

Alpha-acid figures are variety-typical ranges. Always confirm alpha acid on your actual lot before any bittering calculation — this tool covers flavour and aroma character only, not IBU math.
