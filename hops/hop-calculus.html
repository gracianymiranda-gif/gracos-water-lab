Pull a Hop from The Hop Chronicles

The index of 138 posts is built into this file, so looking up a URL works offline — it resolves the variety, crop year, origin, series and publication date with no network at all. Fetching only adds the descriptor ratings and the measured alpha acid of that lot.

Fetching will usually fail. Brülosophy does not send CORS headers, and a browser will not let this page read a cross‑site response without them — that is the site's decision, not something this file can work around. Opening the post and pasting its text does the same job and always works. Nothing is ever saved automatically; every parsed value lands in the form for you to check.

How scores are derived

The series uses a consistent results sentence, so scores come from the post's own ranking rather than a guess:

Where the descriptor appearsScore
1st / 2nd / 3rd in a "most prominent" list9 / 8 / 7
4th or later in that list6/10
Mentioned elsewhere in the post (pasted articles only)5/10
Never mentioned2/10
Named among the "lowest rated" (pasted articles only)1/10

Many posts rank aroma and flavour separately; an axis keeps its best rank across the two lists, which is why two or three axes on one hop can all read 9. The shipped entries were built from those ranked lists alone — the 5 for a passing mention and the 1 for lowest-rated appear only when you paste a whole article, because that is when the rest of the text is available. This is a ranking translated to a scale, not a measurement — Brülosophy publishes descriptor votes, not oil analysis. Two hops both scoring 9 on citrus were each their post's top descriptor; it does not mean they are equally citrusy. Descriptors the series tracks but this tool has no axis for (onion/garlic, apple/pear) are reported as notes instead of being folded into a neighbouring axis.

Import / Export

Export saves your current library (defaults + your edits, and which shipped hops you deleted) to a JSON file you can back up or share. Import replaces the working library with that file, and rejects one whose entries are malformed rather than half-loading it. Shipped hops the file does not contain stay deleted; hops a later data update adds still arrive.

Where each profile comes from. Every comparison shows a badge. from post is first-hand: the chemistry and the ranked descriptors were read out of the Brülosophy article itself. Every Hop Chronicles entry that ships with this tool is this tier, and pasting an article into the Hop Chronicles tab marks anything you add the same way. composite is a generalized variety character — informed judgement about how a hop reads across sources, not a measurement. The twenty non-Chronicles varieties are this tier. yours is whatever you typed under Manage Hops. unscored means no profile exists; the entry is excluded from the radar, the blend average and the ranking. estimate and reported no longer ship. They marked profiles drawn from search-engine summaries rather than the posts, and were replaced when all 138 articles were parsed directly. If you used an earlier version of this page, your saved copy may still carry a few; they are the weakest tier here and worth re-pulling from the article.

Figures from, and shared profiles. Figures from links the article the chemistry and the ranking were read out of. Eleven varieties have two articles (a different crop year or test beer); they share one profile, and the entry whose article it did not come from says so and links the one it did. That is a citation, not a measurement of your lot. Verified is the stronger claim and only you can set it.

Verified vs assumed. Nothing ships verified — only you can attest to a source. When you check a hop against a spec sheet, a lab report or your own lot, tick verified under Manage Hops and name what you checked it against; the entry is then marked ✓ everywhere it appears, and a later data update will never overwrite it with an estimate. The library panel tracks how much of your library is confirmed rather than assumed.

Alpha-acid figures are variety-typical ranges. Always confirm alpha acid on your actual lot before any bittering calculation — this tool covers flavour and aroma character only, not IBU math. Your edits are saved to this browser's local storage, so they persist here on reload but will not sync across devices unless you export/import the JSON.
